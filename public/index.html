About StudySOS

Problem

Students often struggle to manage large syllabi, limited time, difficult subjects, and exam pressure.

Solution

StudySOS acts as an AI academic rescue system that analyzes a student's situation and provides personalized study support.

Impact

It helps students prioritize, plan, understand, practice, and track their preparation.
How the AI works: doubts, notes and quizzes are generated by a backend service that holds the model API key; no keys are ever placed in frontend code. If the AI service is unavailable, StudySOS falls back to built-in offline answers so studying is never blocked.
